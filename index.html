K.A.R.A.O.K.E. — Kick-Ass Realtime Audio → Onstage Karaoke Experience

Any track — a song, a talk, a poem, a voicemail — goes in one end. Seconds later, out the other end: transcribed lyrics, detected chords, a live karaoke page with flames behind the words, a beautiful print-ready PDF, and a link you can share before the next song's intro ends.

1 · LISTENword-by-word transcription, timestamped to the syllable
2 · HEARchord detection from the audio itself — the timeline of the harmony
3 · WEAVEchords pinned to the words exactly where they change
4 · IGNITEthe karaoke page — words light up in flames as they're sung 🔥
5 · PRINTlyrics + chords, stage-ready A4 PDF
